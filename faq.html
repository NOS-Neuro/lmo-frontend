Common questions about AI visibility

Transparent answers about what we do, what we don't do, and how our services work.

What's the difference between the free scan and the $495 Tier 0 snapshot?

The free scan is an automated, AI-assisted assessment that gives you directional scores (Discovery, Accuracy, Authority). It helps you understand IF issues exist, but it's not a comprehensive audit.

Tier 0 ($495 CAD) is a human-reviewed analysis where we manually test your business across ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity, identify confusion patterns, flag accuracy issues, and provide written interpretation and recommendations. It shows you WHAT the problem is, not just that a problem exists.

Does the free scan actually query real AI models?

Yes. The free scan uses real AI APIs to test how models respond to questions about your business. However, it's automated and uses AI-assisted scoring — not comprehensive human review like our paid tiers.

Can you guarantee Wikipedia placement or editing?

No. Wikipedia has strict editorial policies and we cannot guarantee placement, editing success, or that pages will remain live. We can help you understand Wikipedia's requirements and prepare accurate information, but we don't promise outcomes we can't control.

What is a "Canonical Truth File"?

It's a structured, authoritative profile of your business containing all the facts AI systems should know: what you do, where you operate, your specialties, key differentiators, and more. Think of it as the single source of truth that AI can reference instead of guessing or relying on outdated directories.

We build this in Tier 1 and higher. It's stored in a machine-readable format (JSON, schema markup, etc.) that AI systems can reliably access and cite.

How long does it take to see results?

Free scan: Immediate (30-60 seconds)
Tier 0 snapshot: 3-5 business days for human review and written analysis
Tier 1 implementation: 2-4 weeks to build and deploy your canonical profile and schema
Monitoring results: AI training data evolves slowly. Expect meaningful visibility changes over 2-6 months, not overnight.

We track progress monthly and show you how AI responses are changing over time.

Is this the same as SEO?

No. SEO focuses on search engine rankings (Google, Bing). AI visibility focuses on how ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and similar systems describe and recommend you when people ask questions conversationally.

They're complementary. SEO helps people find you via search. AI visibility ensures they get accurate information when they ask AI about you. 40% of prospects now ask AI before visiting websites — that's a different discovery layer than traditional search.

Which AI systems do you test?

Our paid tiers (Tier 0 and higher) include human-reviewed testing across ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity.

The free scan uses AI-assisted estimation and may test a subset of models. It's directional, not comprehensive.

Can you force AI to change what it says about us?

No. We can't "control" AI systems. What we can do:

  • Give AI better, more authoritative data to reference
  • Deploy structured schema so AI has clear facts to cite
  • Submit corrections when AI gets facts demonstrably wrong
  • Monitor how AI descriptions evolve over time

AI systems make their own decisions about what to say. We improve the inputs they use to make those decisions.

What if I already have good SEO? Do I still need this?

Probably yes. SEO helps people find your website in search results. AI visibility ensures that when people ask AI about you directly, they get accurate information.

AI doesn't just link to your site — it describes you in its own words. If that description is wrong, outdated, or confusing, good SEO won't fix it. You need structured, authoritative data that AI can reliably cite.

How often should we monitor AI visibility?

Monthly is standard for active monitoring (included in Tier 1 and above). AI training data evolves gradually, so quarterly checks may be sufficient if your business is stable.

If you're launching new services, rebranding, or have had recent press coverage, more frequent monitoring helps ensure AI picks up the changes correctly.
